# Open-Source Launch Strategy

## Positioning

Use this message:

> A safety-first, read-only prediction-market research kernel for reproducible
> Polymarket/Kalshi mapping, capture, and replay.

Avoid claiming that the bot is profitable or live-ready.

## Timeline

1. Week 1: public truth, docs, safety model, CI, contributor files.
2. Week 2: fixture demo, software health report, mapping-review workflow.
3. Week 3: soft launch to technical reviewers and prediction-market developers.
4. Week 4: public launch after CI, docs, and demo are polished.

## Channels

- GitHub: polished README, badges, issues, roadmap, and good-first-issue labels.
- Hacker News: “Show HN: A read-only research and replay toolkit for prediction markets.”
- Reddit: engineering/reproducibility angle, not trading signals.
- X/LinkedIn: architecture diagram, safety model, and fixture demo.
- Product Hunt: only if the demo is accessible and visually clear.

## Contributor onboarding

Good first issues should focus on:

- docs improvements;
- sanitized fixtures;
- source-contract examples;
- mapping-candidate review checklists;
- replay report visualization.

Do not ask first-time contributors to handle live credentials or trading paths.
