# Code of Conduct

This project follows a simple standard: be respectful, evidence-driven, and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Treat contributors professionally.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Keep claims technically defensible.
- Respect the project’s no-live-trading posture.
- Avoid hype, profit promises, or encouragement to bypass safety gates.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, abuse, or discriminatory conduct.
- Sharing private keys, credentials, or account data.
- Encouraging live trading without the documented gates.
- Presenting unverified strategy results as profit proof.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, close, or remove issues, comments, or pull requests that violate
this code. Serious or repeated violations can result in a ban from project spaces.
