# Open-Source Readiness Report

Generated for the profitability-proof and flagship open-source rebuild.

## Current supported claim

The repository can claim that the read-only research/paper infrastructure is working
when the software health report passes.

It cannot currently claim that a strategy is profitable or live-ready.

## Implemented

- Read-only research kernel under `research/`.
- Source-contract fixture validation under `research_contracts/`.
- Candidate generation with candidates-only output.
- Manual mapping catalog policy under `research_mappings/catalog.yaml`.
- Append-only capture/replay machinery.
- Software-health reporting command.
- Public proof standard and safety documentation.
- Research-safe `.env.example`.
- Open-source contributor, governance, roadmap, CI, and template scaffolding.

## Blocked before profitability proof

- At least 10 manually approved mappings.
- 14 calendar days of forward paper capture.
- Dynamic fee metadata resolved per accepted observation.
- Mapping-clustered or moving-block bootstrap confidence interval.
- Positive conservative lower confidence bound after all costs.

## Blocked before live readiness

- Jurisdiction-specific legal/compliance clearance.
- Explicit live canary approval.
- Isolated live credentials.
- Canary size, max daily loss, kill switch, and rollback plan.
- Current official venue API audit of all execution paths.

## Weak claims retired

- Reward farming is not live-ready.
- AI/news trading is not profit-proven.
- Whale tracking is not profit-proven.
- Latency-sensitive market making remains paused.
